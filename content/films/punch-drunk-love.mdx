This is one of the films I think people absolutely should see. What holds me is
Barry's habit of hiding his awkwardness, his flaws, the parts of himself he finds
embarrassing, from the very person he loves. He seems unusual at first. I suspect
he resembles people we meet every day. The contradiction is what draws me in:
wanting to love someone, and at the same time being reluctant to show them who
you really are. For me the film goes beyond romance. It asks a more basic
question, about what it means to love.
