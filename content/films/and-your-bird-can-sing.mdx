I am drawn to the hopeful, captivating way Miyake portrays young adults in
contemporary Japan. He acknowledges the uncertainty, anxiety, and aimlessness
that can characterize their lives, yet he never reduces his characters to those
struggles. What interests me is his fascination with young people themselves,
and the way he observes how they live. There is an openness and an affection in
his perspective that I find compelling.
