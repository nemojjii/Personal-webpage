I don't see this merely as a story about inheriting a throne. What I see is a
young person confronting established customs, inherited expectations, and a
social order shaped by the generations before him, while trying to remain
faithful to his own convictions. The film makes me think about how strength need
not be defined by physical power. Conviction, integrity, and the determination to
uphold one's principles can be forms of strength as well. That, at least, is how
I read it.
