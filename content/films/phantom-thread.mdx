Daniel Day-Lewis had announced that this would be his last film, and at the time
that alone was a compelling reason to see it. What interests me more is the story
itself: a man whose work, habits, and carefully maintained order are disturbed
by love. I find the simultaneous presence of unsettling emotion and unexpected
humor especially interesting. The film portrays love as something more
complicated than beauty or tenderness. Control, obsession, dependence, and
transformation all live inside it at once.
