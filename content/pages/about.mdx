I am an undergraduate at Rutgers University, studying chemical physics and
mathematics.

## Academic background

Chemical physics and mathematics, taken together on purpose. The two are less
separate than a course catalogue makes them look: much of what makes a physical
system tractable is a choice of mathematical structure, and much of what makes a
structure worth studying is that something physical turns out to obey it.

## Research interests

Theoretical science and mathematical modeling — in particular the structures
underneath physical systems, and what it means when a model built for one
phenomenon turns out to describe another it was never written for. The work I
have done so far runs from signal analysis to network dynamics to gravitational
waves; the thread through it is modeling rather than any one system.

## Intellectual interests

Philosophy, literature, and the question of how we come to perceive and express
anything at all. These are not kept at a distance from the science. They are
usually where my questions about it begin, and occasionally where they end up.

## On learning and research

A result that has been verified is not the same as a result that has been
understood, and the second takes considerably longer. This site is arranged
around that difference: research for what can be stated, projects and the
notebook for what is still being worked out.
