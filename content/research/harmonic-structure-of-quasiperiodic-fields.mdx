Consider the field obtained by superposing $N$ plane waves of equal magnitude,
with directions evenly spaced over a half turn:

$$
\psi_N(\mathbf{p}) \;=\; \frac{1}{N} \sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
\cos\!\left(\kappa\,\mathbf{e}(\theta_j)\cdot \mathbf{p} + \varphi_j\right),
\qquad \theta_j = \frac{\pi j}{N}.
$$

For $N = 2, 3, 4, 6$ the result is periodic. For $N = 5$ it is not: the
directions generate a rank-four module over $\Z$, and $\psi_5$ is quasiperiodic,
with local five-fold symmetry and no translation symmetry at all.
