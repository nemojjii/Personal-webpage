A preview, not a finding. This entry exists to mark that the exploration has
begun — not to report what it has found, because nothing here is settled
enough yet to report.

The questions worth asking first: what changes, mathematically, when a
viewpoint moves rather than sits still — and whether the structures used to
describe that change have anything useful to say about how a landscape is
built, in the mathematical sense, out of what is seen from it.

More will be written here as the exploration itself becomes something more
definite than a set of questions.
