As the Mechanical Lead of FTC Team 26039, αsymptote, I focused primarily on the
robot's mechanical mechanisms, and took part in design reviews and development
decisions alongside that work.

## The approach

The project involved collaborative engineering within the constraints of
competitive robotics — mechanical problem-solving, iterative design and
practical implementation, carried out as a team.

## The outcome

During the 2024–2025 INTO THE DEEP season the team competed at the New York –
Long Island Championship. Its achievements there included second place for the
Innovate Award sponsored by RTX and a place in the Finalist Alliance as its
first selected team. These were the team's achievements.

The project represents an earlier exploration of mechanical engineering through
collaborative design and competition.
