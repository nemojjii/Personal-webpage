*This page is a place to work, not a finding. Everything here is provisional.*

Perception is comparative before it is anything else: we notice that one thing
differs from another, and only later ask by how much. That is already close to
the language of mathematics — difference, distance, invariance — and the
closeness is what interests me.

What I am less sure of is the second half. An artist choosing a colour or an
interval is not measuring a distance; they are committing to one possibility and
giving up the others. Whether those two things — comparison and commitment —
belong in the same description, or only sound as though they do, is the question
I keep returning to.
